# CampusFest growth strategy: fact-check sources

| 1 | DU has 77 colleges, about 1,32,435 regular students | PARTLY (out of date) | The official DU page says 77 colleges and 1,32,435 regular students (UG 1,14,494; PG 17,941), plus 2,61,169 in non-formal education. The page was last updated in 2020. A Sept 2026 news report gives 91 colleges, but it is not official. Suggested wording: "77 colleges and 1.32 lakh regular students (DU website)". | University of Delhi, Academics page, https://www.du.ac.in/index.php?page=academics-2 ; also News24, "Delhi University hostel crisis: 71 of 91 colleges have no hostels", https://news24online.com/india/delhi-university-hostel-crisis-71-of-91-colleges-have-no-hostels-leaving-thousands-of-students-dependent-on-outside-pgs-and-rented-rooms/920417/ | DU page last updated 31 Dec 2020; News24 9 Sep 2026 |
| 2 | The Magenta Line serves JNU, IIT Delhi and Jamia, and is called the "knowledge corridor" | VERIFIED | DMRC called the Magenta Line the "knowledge corridor". It links JNU, IIT Delhi, Jamia Millia Islamia and Amity (Noida). Stations: IIT (named for the institute), Munirka (about 2 km from JNU), Jamia Millia Islamia. JNU has no station of its own. | Business Standard (PTI), "DMRC terms Magenta Line as 'knowledge corridor'", https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/dmrc-terms-magenta-line-as-knowledge-corridor-118052401028_1.html ; Millennium Post, "Delhi Metro's new Magenta line to connect four major universities", https://www.millenniumpost.in/delhi/delhi-metros-new-magenta-line-to-connect-four-major-universities-301154 | 24 May 2018 |
| 3 | Delhi crowd-safety guidance says online registration should be preferred to avoid overcrowding | VERIFIED (the source is Delhi Police, not NDMA) | Delhi Police SOP for college fests, item 1(xii): "Online registration should be preferred to mitigate overcrowding and consequential stampede like situation at the venue." The Delhi High Court reproduced the SOP in its order closing the suo motu PIL. The NDMA guide (2014, s. 6.3.3) only says online registration "could be used to influence the arrival pattern". | Delhi High Court order with the Delhi Police SOP (PDF via LiveLaw), https://www.livelaw.in/pdf_upload/mmh22042024cw132932023190030-535598.pdf ; LiveLaw report, https://livelaw.in/high-court/delhi-high-court/delhi-police-prepares-sop-for-ensuring-students-safety-during-college-fests-high-court-closes-suo-motu-pil-255939 ; NDMA, "Managing Crowd at Events and Venues of Mass Gathering", https://sdma.goa.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-05/managingcrowdsguide.pdf | Order 22 Apr 2024; LiveLaw 24 Apr 2024; NDMA guide 2014 |
| 4 | Karnataka crowd control bill (2025) requires police permission for events of 5,000+ | VERIFIED (it is still a Bill) | Organisers must get permission for events with 5,000 or more people. The permitting officer depends on crowd size: station officer-in-charge (5,000-7,000), DySP (7,000-50,000), SP or Commissioner (above 50,000). Applications are due 10 days before the event. The Bill was introduced in Aug 2025 and sent to a Select Committee, so write "would require", not "requires". | PRS Legislative Research, State Legislative Brief: The Karnataka Crowd Control Bill, 2025, https://prsindia.org/bills/state-legislative-briefs/the-karnataka-crowd-control-management-of-crowds-at-events-and-places-of-assembly-bill-2025 | Bill introduced Aug 2025 |

| 6 | Rendezvous 2023: gate trouble and a stampede-like situation, concerts affected; 1,60,000+ footfall over 4 days, 20,000+ participants | PARTLY | The 2023 incident is correct. No source found for 20,000+ participants; drop it, or say "students from 1,000+ colleges". | Careers360, "IIT Delhi students 'manhandled' during campus festival, admin cancels Sonu Nigam, Guru Randhawa's live concert", https://news.careers360.com/iit-delhi-students-manhandled-campus-rendezvous-admin-cancels-sonu-nigam-guru-randhawas-live-concert ; Rendezvous official site, https://rdv-iitd.org ; Wikipedia, "Rendezvous (festival)", https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendezvous_(festival) | Careers360 13 Mar 2023; site figures for Sep 2025 |
